## Engineering internal operations

The Internal Backoffice is an enterprise application developed to support operational and administrative workflows at United Capital.

The platform brings together different business processes within a centralized interface, helping internal teams manage requests, access operational information, and work with structured workflows.

## What the platform supports

The application includes business areas related to:

- Internal request management and tracking.
- Administrative configuration of request types and requirements.
- Workflow-driven operational processes.
- Historical records and process visibility.
- Interfaces for interacting with internal business services.

These capabilities are described at a general level to respect the confidentiality of internal operations.

## My contribution

As part of the engineering team, my work involves developing and maintaining application functionality, implementing user interfaces, integrating business services, and supporting the evolution of internal workflows.

The project involves working with existing enterprise architecture while balancing maintainability, operational requirements, and user experience.

## Engineering environment

The application uses Next.js, React, and TypeScript, with a modular structure organized around business domains.

Its frontend architecture incorporates application state management, asynchronous data handling, and integrations with enterprise services.

## Professional context

Working on internal enterprise applications requires understanding business processes, translating operational requirements into software, and maintaining systems that support day-to-day activities.

This project reflects my experience building software within an established financial organization and collaborating on systems used for internal operations.
