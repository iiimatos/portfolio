## Bringing visibility to document workflows

DocuTracker is a document tracking and transfer system developed during my time at the Liga Municipal Dominicana.

The application was designed to support administrative document workflows, providing a structured way to manage document movements and improve visibility throughout their lifecycle.

The project reflects the importance of building reliable internal tools that simplify everyday operations and support institutional processes.

## The challenge

Administrative environments often involve documents moving between different departments, teams, and responsible individuals.

Without a centralized system, maintaining visibility over document movements and understanding their current status can become challenging.

DocuTracker addresses this problem by providing a digital environment for organizing and tracking document transfers.

## Core capabilities

The system focuses on several areas of document management:

- Registration and organization of administrative documents.
- Tracking document transfers between departments.
- Visibility into document status and movement history.
- Structured management of document-related information.
- Support for internal administrative workflows.

These capabilities are presented at a general level without exposing internal institutional processes.

## Engineering approach

DocuTracker combines a modern web interface with backend services designed to support document-related operations.

The technology stack includes:

- **Next.js:** Web application development and user interfaces.
- **C# and .NET:** Backend development and business operations.
- **SQL Server:** Relational data persistence.

The project required translating administrative requirements into application functionality while maintaining clear interactions between frontend components, backend services, and persistent data.

As a Full-Stack Developer, I contributed to the development of the application across different layers of the system.
