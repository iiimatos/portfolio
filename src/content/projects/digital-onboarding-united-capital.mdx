## Transforming the client onboarding experience

Digital Onboarding is an enterprise application developed at United Capital to support the digital registration and onboarding of financial clients.

The platform is designed to simplify how individuals and businesses initiate their relationship with the organization, replacing fragmented processes with a more structured and accessible digital experience.

The project combines frontend development, backend engineering, identity verification, document management, and integrations with enterprise systems.

## The challenge

Client onboarding in the financial sector involves more than collecting personal information.

Applications must coordinate multiple stages of information gathering, identity verification, document submission, validation, and internal processing.

These workflows become more complex when supporting different customer profiles, such as individuals and legal entities.

The engineering challenge is to provide a clear user experience while supporting the business rules, validations, and operational requirements of a regulated financial environment.

## Core capabilities

The platform supports digital onboarding processes involving:

- Registration workflows for individuals and legal entities.
- Collection and validation of client information.
- Identity verification and supporting documentation.
- Document submission and management.
- Multi-step forms with contextual validations.
- Integration with enterprise services.
- Coordination between digital registration and internal processing workflows.

The public description intentionally excludes confidential business rules, verification procedures, and internal system details.

## Engineering approach

The solution combines frontend and backend technologies to support complex onboarding workflows.

The technology stack includes:

- **Next.js and React:** Interactive client-facing applications.
- **TypeScript:** Type-safe application development.
- **NestJS:** Backend services and application logic.
- **Enterprise integrations:** Communication with existing business platforms and services.

The application requires coordination between user-facing interactions, backend validations, external services, and document-related operations.

## Frontend engineering

A significant part of the onboarding experience involves guiding users through structured processes without overwhelming them.

The frontend focuses on:

- Multi-step registration experiences.
- Form validation and error handling.
- Conditional interfaces based on customer profiles.
- Document upload interactions.
- Clear feedback throughout the registration process.
- Responsive and accessible user interfaces.

The goal is to make complex financial onboarding requirements easier to navigate.

## Backend and integrations

The backend supports application workflows, data validation, and communication with enterprise systems.

Key engineering considerations include:

- Separation of business logic from transport and infrastructure concerns.
- Validation of incoming application data.
- Coordination with external services.
- Document-related operations.
- Consistent handling of application errors.
- Maintainable integrations with existing systems.
