## Building a better billing experience

Mingo Billing is a web-first billing and commercial management product I'm building as part of the Mingo ecosystem.

The goal is to help businesses manage their customers, products, services, invoices, and financial operations through a unified and intuitive experience.

## Product vision

The planned product capabilities include:

- Customer and business profile management.
- Product and service catalogs.
- Invoice creation, tracking, and document generation.
- Tax configuration and billing workflows.
- Payment tracking and accounts receivable.
- Financial reports and operational visibility.
- Future integration with Dominican electronic invoicing requirements (e-CF/DGII), subject to the necessary regulatory and certification processes.

## Engineering approach

Mingo Billing is being designed around a web-first architecture using Next.js and TypeScript, with a dedicated backend planned for its business operations.

The product is intended to integrate with Mingo's shared identity, organization, and subscription infrastructure while keeping its billing domain independent.

## Current status

The product is in development. Its initial direction and integration with the Mingo ecosystem have been defined, while billing workflows and broader capabilities are being developed progressively.

The roadmap describes the intended scope, not a list of completed features.
