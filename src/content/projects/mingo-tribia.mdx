## Making independent work easier to manage

Mingo Tribia is a product I'm planning for independent professionals and individuals managing their own economic activities in the Dominican Republic.

Its purpose is to make financial and tax-related organization more accessible, helping users maintain clearer records without relying on scattered spreadsheets, documents, and manual processes.

## The problem

Independent professionals often need to organize income, expenses, receipts, and supporting documents before sharing information with their accountants.

Mingo Tribia aims to simplify this preparation and give individuals a clearer view of their financial activity.

## Planned experience

The initial product scope focuses on:

- Recording and categorizing income and expenses.
- Organizing receipts and supporting documents.
- Preparing structured exports for accountants.
- Generating temporary, read-only links to share selected records with accountants.
- Maintaining a personal financial history in one place.

The product is designed to complement professional accounting services, not replace them.

## Product architecture

Mingo Tribia is planned as a personal-account product within the Mingo ecosystem.

Its identity, sessions, and data will remain independent from business organization accounts, while benefiting from shared platform infrastructure where appropriate.

## Current status

Mingo Tribia is currently in the concept and planning stage. Its initial scope and target audience have been defined, with implementation and technical decisions to follow.
